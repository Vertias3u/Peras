Know how much room you have left.

Peras shows how much of your Claude and Codex allowances you've used. Your provider sets the allowance; Peras decides when your agents keep working.

Two windows, each with its own reset.

5h · short window

Your current session allowance

18% means 18% used, about 82% left. It measures usage, not hours: heavy tasks can use it up well before five hours have passed.

The menu bar percentages show this window, with its reset time beside the bar.

week · long window

Your weekly allowance

The weekly bar tracks the longer allowance. A fresh 5-hour window can still be held back by a used-up week.

The weekly bar shows when that allowance resets.

At your line, one of three things happens.

warn

You get told you crossed a line. The agent keeps working; a warning never pauses it.

ask

The next guarded tool action needs your approval. In Codex, which has no approval prompt, it waits until you resume.

pause

The next guarded tool action is blocked. An action already running may finish first. Resuming doesn't refill your allowance.

Weekly pace

Compares how much of the week you've used with how far through it you are, once its minimum usage is reached. It can warn before any fixed percentage line.

Pause 1h · Pause

Pauses the agent for an hour, or until you resume.

Resume 1h · Resume

Lifts the current stop for an hour, or until the window resets. The provider's own limits still apply.

Reading the menu.

Claude orange and Codex blue show used allowance; the empty part is what's left. Red means usage reached one of your pause or ask lines.

The mascots walk along the 5-hour bars and stand still when an agent is paused or waiting for approval. Where they stand is decoration; the fill is the usage.

A dash means Peras has no live reading for that window. It doesn't mean zero usage. Open Status for the reading's age, warnings and refresh errors.

Is the guard actually running?

Protection active

A tool guard ran recently with the current setup. It doesn't prove every open session is covered.

Not recently verified

No live tool guard or successful protection self-test in the last 15 minutes. Run a tool or Protection test to refresh previously verified protection.

Needs trust / activation

Codex needs you to review Peras in /hooks, or a tool call is needed to confirm it. Restart the session after changing hooks; for Claude, restart Claude Code.

Setup missing

The guard is absent, wired wrong, or its runtime is gone. Run setup again.

Preferences.

Under Settings → Menu bar display, turn the mascots and weekly bars on or off independently. Under Settings → Notifications, choose warning, pause/approval and reset alerts. Warning and pause alerts start on; reset alerts start off, need the menu bar running, and use the provider's reported reset time.

Turning notifications off doesn't turn off pausing. A timed pause shows its end time and a countdown, with Resume right below.

Test protection. See what happened.

Choose Settings → Protection test… in the pear menu to test both hook commands with disposable state: allowed, denied while paused, then allowed after resume. Results appear in Status. Your working agents stay as they are, and the test makes no model calls.

A successful self-test changes Not recently verified to Protection active for 15 minutes when the previously verified setup is unchanged. The health details in Status identify the self-test and show the last live tool guard. The test doesn't establish trust or activation in every open session.

Settings → Recent events… shows the latest 50 local summaries of warnings, pauses, resumes, resets and tests. Events are saved even when notifications are off. History starts after this update and contains no prompts or tool commands.
