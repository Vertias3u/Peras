At your line, one of three things happens.

warn

A desktop notification. The agent keeps going.

ask

The next tool call waits for your OK, once per session. Even from your phone.

pause

Tool calls are refused and the turn ends, until it lifts or you run peras resume.

One prompt eats 3% of the weekperas set prompt 3 pause
Overnight runs, at 50%peras set 01:00-08:00 weekly 50 pause
Just this folder, at 60%peras set --here weekly 60 pause

Allowances count observed increases in your weekly percentage. Daily tracking begins with the first reading each day. Reminders require the menu bar and a fresh reading. When one agent pauses, the menu can point out fresh allowance on the other.

Codex gets the same guard.

One command adds the same hooks to Codex desktop and the Codex CLI. Its own status, lines, pauses, allowances and history work there too: peras codex status, peras codex pause, and the rest.

$ peras codex setup
Its own lines

Codex's lines, history and pauses are kept apart from Claude Code's.

No tokens touched

Usage comes from your ChatGPT plan, read through Codex's own local app-server. Peras reads no login files.

Ask means wait

Codex has no approval prompt, so an ask line holds its tools until you run peras codex resume.

Ask it what's going on.

peras whyWhich line is acting, and when it lifts.
peras historyPast weeks: the peak, the lines crossed, and when it hit the wall.
peras pause 1h · peras pause 18:00Pause by hand, for a while or until a time.
peras resume 1hLift a stop for an hour instead of until the reset.
peras eventsRecent warnings, pauses, resumes and resets.
peras testTries a pause and resume on a throwaway copy of the hooks, for both agents.
peras doctorChecks that each agent's hooks actually ran lately.
peras notify resets onChoose your desktop notifications: warnings, pauses, resets.
peras updateThe newest Peras, with your hooks and menu bar refreshed. Your lines and history stay.
peras removeTakes out Claude's hooks and restores its status line; peras codex remove does Codex. Your rules and history stay.

Everything stays on your machine. The event log holds no prompts and no tool commands.
